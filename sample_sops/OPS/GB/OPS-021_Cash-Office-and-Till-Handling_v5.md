# Till floats
Each till opens with the float agreed in the Cash Office. Two colleagues count and sign the float sheet before trading.

# Cash pick-ups
When a till holds more than the pick-up limit, call the cash office on the radio for a pick-up. Never carry cash across the shop floor alone.
Cash office doors must stay locked and the CCTV monitor visible to the manager.

# Banking
Banking is prepared by two colleagues and collected by the approved cash-in-transit company. Do not discuss banking times or routes with customers.
