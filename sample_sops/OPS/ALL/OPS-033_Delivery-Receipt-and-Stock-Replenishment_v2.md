# Receiving a delivery
Two colleagues must be present to receive a delivery. Check the number of cartons against the delivery note before the driver leaves.
Check cartons for visible damage and tampering. Photograph any damage before opening.
Sign the delivery note only after the carton count is confirmed. If cartons are missing, write the shortfall on the delivery note before signing.

# Reporting missing or damaged cartons
Report missing or damaged cartons to the Stock Control Desk within 24 hours using the Delivery Discrepancy form.
Attach the delivery note and photographs. Keep damaged stock separate in the stockroom quarantine bay until Stock Control confirms the outcome.

# Replenishing the shop floor
Replenish from the stockroom using the replenishment list printed at opening. Priority is given to sizes that are out of stock on the shop floor.
Never leave cages or cartons in fire exit routes or aisles. Cages must be returned to the stockroom when empty.
Use the correct manual handling technique. Items over 15 kg require two colleagues or a trolley.
