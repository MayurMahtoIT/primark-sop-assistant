# Purpose
This SOP sets out how colleagues keep fitting rooms safe and how they respond when a customer needs help.

# Fitting room checks
Colleagues must walk the fitting room area at least every 30 minutes and record the check on the Store Safety Log.
Remove any clothing left on floors immediately because it is a slip and trip hazard.
Report damaged doors, locks or mirrors to the manager on duty and close the affected cubicle until it is repaired.

# Customer unwell or injured in a fitting room
Stay calm and speak to the customer through the door. Do not force the door unless there is an immediate risk to life.
Call the manager on duty on the store radio and request a first aider.
If the customer does not respond or the situation is serious, call the emergency services on 999 or 112 and ask a colleague to meet them at the main entrance.
The first aider leads care. Do not move an injured customer unless they are in danger.
Complete an Accident Report Form the same day and give it to the store manager. The manager records the incident on the Safety Portal within 24 hours.

# Capacity
The fitting room attendant limits customers to the item count shown on the sign. Unattended children must not be left alone in a cubicle.
